I currently serve as **Technology Director at JMA Integra**, leading cybersecurity and AI initiatives from strategy and architecture through delivery, governance, and executive communication. My career spans Spain, Andorra, Ireland, and Mexico, combining hands-on technical depth with leadership of secure, business-aligned technology programs.
